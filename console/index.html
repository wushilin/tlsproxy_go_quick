Certificates

Certificate authorities

A rule terminates TLS with one of these by cert = local-ca:ca002 (plain cert = local-ca is ca001). Certificates are issued the first time a client asks for a name, kept in the CA's folder, and renewed automatically; anything in a folder that the CA did not issue is moved to its _archive and deleted after 14 days. A self-signed CA is renewed with the same key, so what it issued stays valid. Clients must trust the CA: download its certificate here. Private keys never leave the proxy.

Generate a CA

    

    

      
      
      
      
      
    

    
These are fixed once the CA exists (they are written to its config.toml, whose [ca] section can't change them). It is renewed with the same key before it expires. What it issues is set in [certificates], editable later with Settings.

    

    

      
      
    

  




  

    
Import a CA

    

    
    
    
Checked before anything is stored: it must be a CA allowed to sign certificates, the key must be its own, and a chain must be in order. Certificates it issues are sent with its chain (without the root), so clients that trust your root accept them.

    

    

      
      
    

  




  

    

      
CA settings

      
    

    
[certificates] says what this CA issues, and is read again as soon as it is saved: new certificates and renewals follow it; existing ones keep theirs until renewed. [ca] describes how the CA was made and can't be changed.
